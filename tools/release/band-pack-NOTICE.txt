YourBand band pack: what is inside, and under which terms
==========================================================

Band\band.bundle holds the game's four 3D musicians, their instruments and their animations. It is an optional
part of YourBand (https://github.com/barsk/YourBand); the game works without it, minus the band view (V).

  The musicians       Microsoft Rocketbox avatars (https://github.com/microsoft/Microsoft-Rocketbox),
                      MIT licence: the notice is at the end of this file.
  Their playing       Animations from Adobe Mixamo (https://www.mixamo.com), used under Mixamo's terms as part of
                      an application: they are not offered on their own.
  The instruments     3D models from GrabCAD's community library (https://grabcad.com/library): an Eric Clapton
                      Stratocaster, a Precision Bass, a Sonor drum set and a Yamaha EZ-220 keyboard, modified for
                      the game; none came with licence terms, or the terms were permissive.

The full list with sources is CREDITS.md in the repository. The pack's own parts (materials, animator controllers,
the way the musicians play) are part of YourBand, under the GNU General Public License v3.
